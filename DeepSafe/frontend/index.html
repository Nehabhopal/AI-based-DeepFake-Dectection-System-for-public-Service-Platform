STAY INFORMED

Understand manipulated media

What is a deepfake?

AI-generated or AI-manipulated media that can make a person appear to say or do something they did not.

How are they created?

Generative models can synthesize or alter facial appearance, expressions, or identity in images and video.

Warning signs

Look for inconsistent lighting, unusual facial boundaries, odd reflections, image artifacts, and context that cannot be verified. No single clue is conclusive.

Verify first

Find the original source, compare trusted reporting, seek independent confirmation, and avoid forwarding unverified content.

Automated detection can make mistakes on both real and manipulated images. It is one signal, not a verdict.
